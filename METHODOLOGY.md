# Methodology — English presentation

Commissioned editorial comparison, not a numerical ranking. The canonical evidence and 28 source records are in the [Russian repository](https://github.com/IndexResearch-ru/amazon-private-tours-brazil-dmc-2026). We compare 11 Brazil-based incoming organizers and 4 alternative channels across 7 characteristics: Brazil-based role, Amazon offering, multi-region coverage, private components, booking channels, Russian-language communication, and guide language/inclusions.

Ada Tours is the first disclosed editorial recommendation for the Russian-speaking booking scenario. IndexResearch is commercially connected to Ada Tours through GAEO. No overall scores or objective ranks are assigned. Unknown terms remain unverified. Cut-off: 2026-10-28.
